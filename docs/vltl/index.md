# Video Lap Tracking Lab

[Video Lap Tracking Lab](pathname:///vltl/) (VLTL) runs in your browser. Load your FPV video, mark each lap frame by frame, and it finds your best single lap and your best 3 consecutive laps. Nothing is uploaded: the video stays on your computer.

<a className="button button--primary" href="pathname:///vltl/" target="_self">Open the lap timer</a>

## Guides

- [Lap timer overlay for DaVinci Resolve](./resolve-guide.mdx): copy one script from the lap timer and get live lap timers on your footage, plus the HDZero and Betaflight OSD fonts.

Hi, I'm Max (Maximilian Degen), racing as **MAXDAX**. I'm an IT apprentice and a competitive FPV racing pilot from the Stuttgart area in Germany. I have been racing since 2023 and finished 18th of 106 pilots at the MultiGP European Championship 2026.

Besides flying, I design and build race gates, run race direction and timing at club and national events, and build free tools for FPV pilots. This site collects my tools, their documentation, my projects and my race results.

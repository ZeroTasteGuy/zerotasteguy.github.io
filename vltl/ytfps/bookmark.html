VLTL FPS bookmark (test)

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

VLTL FPS

  1. Open the test lap timer and paste a YouTube link into the URL box (load it).
  2. Click the bookmark. It opens that video on YouTube.
  3. Click the bookmark again once the video has loaded. It reads the frame rate from YouTube's own page data and brings you back to the lap timer with that video loaded and the frame rate set.
